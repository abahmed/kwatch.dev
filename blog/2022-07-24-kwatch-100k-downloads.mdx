**We are glad to announce that [kwatch](https://github.com/abahmed/kwatch) hit [+100k downloads](https://github.com/abahmed/kwatch/pkgs/container/kwatch) 🚀 since its release!**

<!--truncate-->

> This post records a historical adoption milestone from July 2022. The
> product and supported installation workflow have continued to evolve since
> then; start with the current [getting started guide](/docs) when trying kwatch.

**kwatch** helps teams see what broke, understand why, and know what to do next
when a Kubernetes workload fails. It sends incident context to Slack, Discord,
PagerDuty, email, webhooks, and many other channels.

First of all, Big thanks to our **contributors** for their contributions to improving and implementing new features in kwatch
