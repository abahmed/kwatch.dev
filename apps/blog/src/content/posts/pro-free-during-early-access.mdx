Every Pro license is free during early access. Here is what Community gives you, what Pro adds, and how to get a key.

## What Community gives you

kwatch Community names what is failing and the type of cause. It is free forever, with no account and no key.

## What Pro adds

Pro adds the full chain of cause, the next command to run, who made the change, what else is hit, the logs that prove it, and dedicated support.

## Get a license

Sign in at [app.kwatch.dev](https://app.kwatch.dev) to get a key. Then run the installer, open License / Pro and choose Add or replace key.

```bash
bash <(curl -fsSL https://kwatch.dev/kwatch.sh)
```

Each license is free for 3 months and may be renewed. One license runs one cluster.

## Your data

Pro checks the license once a day. The check sends the license ID, a hashed cluster ID, the kwatch version and the node count. It sends nothing else.

## If a license ends

kwatch falls back to Community and your alerts keep coming.
