# 📄 Licensing

Kwatch is free and open source under the
[MIT License](https://github.com/abahmed/kwatch/blob/main/LICENSE). The license permits personal and commercial use,
modification, distribution, and redistribution while requiring the copyright
and license notice to be preserved.

All capabilities included in a release are available under the same terms.
Runtime configuration can enable or disable monitors and individual signals;
these controls are operational settings only.

Third-party dependencies keep their own licenses and are listed in
[third-party notices](/docs/third-party-notices).
