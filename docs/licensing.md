# 📄 Licensing

kwatch is source available under the
[Elastic License 2.0 (ELv2)](https://github.com/abahmed/kwatch/blob/main/LICENSE),
starting after `v1.0.0-rc.10`.

ELv2 lets you use kwatch for free, including commercially in your own
clusters, and read, modify and redistribute the code. It has three
limitations:

1. You may not provide kwatch to others as a hosted or managed service.
2. You may not move, change, disable or circumvent license key
   functionality.
3. You may not remove or obscure licensing, copyright or other notices.

The [Community edition](/docs/editions) is free with no account or license
key. [Pro](/pricing) features are enabled with a license key, one per cluster.

## Earlier releases

Releases up to and including `v1.0.0-rc.10` were published under the
[MIT License](https://github.com/abahmed/kwatch/blob/v1.0.0-rc.10/LICENSE),
and those releases remain available under MIT.

of kwatch.

[third-party notices](/docs/third-party-notices).
